Glucose meters and CGM

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

Understanding readings and trends.

  • A glucose meter tests a drop of blood. Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) tracks glucose in interstitial fluid.
  • CGM also shows trends, but readings may differ from finger-stick results during rapid changes.
  • If a CGM reading does not match how you feel, follow device instructions and your diabetes team's guidance on checking with a meter.
  • Targets, alerts and responses to readings should be personalized with a clinician.
Sources: https://professional.diabetes.org/standards-of-care ↗
