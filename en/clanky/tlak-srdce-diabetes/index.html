Diabetes and blood pressure: look beyond glucose

Blood vessel care includes glucose, blood pressure and blood fats. High blood pressure often causes no pain, so regular checks matter.

Published: 13 November 2026

Educational content only; it does not replace individual advice from a healthcare professional.

Consider the whole picture

Diabetes can increase cardiovascular risk. Good glucose management does not replace blood pressure and cholesterol checks. Targets are individual and depend on overall health.

Home measurements provide information

Use a suitable upper-arm monitor with the correct cuff size. Measure after resting quietly and follow the technique demonstrated by your healthcare professional. Record results and circumstances; a single high reading does not establish a diagnosis.

Habits and medicines both matter

Regular activity suited to your abilities, not smoking and avoiding excess salt can help. Prescribed medicines have their own role. Do not stop them because you feel well or adjust the dose yourself.

Symptoms that cannot wait

Sudden chest pain or pressure, breathlessness, a drooping face, limb weakness or speech difficulty need immediate help. Call 112 in the EU, or the local emergency number elsewhere. Do not wait to check every reading and do not drive yourself.
