Long-term complications: what can you influence?

Regular checkups and individualized treatment help reduce the risk of diabetes-related complications.

Published: 31 August 2026

Educational content only; it does not replace individual advice from a healthcare professional.

Why checkups matter

Persistently high blood glucose can damage small and large blood vessels and nerves. Blood pressure, blood lipids, smoking, and other factors also affect risk.

Eyes, kidneys, and nerves

Retinal examinations, kidney function checks, and foot assessments are scheduled according to diabetes type, duration, and individual risk. Your healthcare team will advise on timing.

Heart and blood vessels

Alongside blood glucose, monitor blood pressure, cholesterol, physical activity, and sleep, and avoid smoking. Treatment targets are individualized.

A practical plan

Keep track of preventive appointments, test results, and questions for your clinician. Do not delay seeking advice about new symptoms.
