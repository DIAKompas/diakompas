Movement and diabetes

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

Making movement part of everyday life.

  • Regular movement can benefit glucose levels, cardiovascular health, sleep and wellbeing.
  • For most adults with diabetes, professional standards recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate-to-vigorous aerobic activity per week, adapted to health and ability.
  • Walking, swimming, cycling and strength training may all help; build up gradually.
  • With insulin or medication that can cause hypoglycemia, activity may lower glucose. Discuss a safety plan with your clinician.
Sources: https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S005 ↗
