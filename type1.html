Type 1 diabetes

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

What happens in the body and why insulin is essential.

  • Type 1 diabetes is an autoimmune condition in which the immune system damages insulin-producing pancreatic cells.
  • Possible symptoms include excessive thirst, frequent urination, fatigue and unintentional weight loss. They may develop quickly.
  • People with type 1 diabetes need insulin. Dosing and treatment changes should be guided by their clinical team.
  • Vomiting, abdominal pain, deep breathing, confusion or marked drowsiness may signal ketoacidosis and require emergency care.
Sources: https://professional.diabetes.org/standards-of-care ↗
