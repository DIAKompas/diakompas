Type 2 diabetes

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

How it develops and why long-term care matters.

  • In type 2 diabetes the body does not use insulin effectively and may eventually produce too little.
  • The condition may develop without obvious symptoms. Diagnosis requires clinical laboratory testing.
  • Care can include balanced nutrition, movement, sleep, medication and regular checkups tailored to the person.
  • Type 2 diabetes is not a personal failure. Genetics and other factors also affect risk.
