Eating with diabetes

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

Practical principles without unnecessary restrictions.

  • No single meal plan works for everyone. Food choices should fit treatment, preferences and other health needs.
  • Vegetables, whole grains, suitable protein sources, unsaturated fats and appropriate portions are useful foundations.
  • Carbohydrates affect glucose levels; some treatment plans require attention to their amount and timing.
  • If you use insulin or medication that can cause low glucose, discuss major dietary changes with your diabetes team.
Sources: https://professional.diabetes.org/standards-of-care ↗
