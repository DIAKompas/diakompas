Diabetic foot care

Editorial draft · 14 Oct 2026

Spotting changes early can prevent complications.

  • Diabetes can affect nerves and blood vessels in the feet. Injuries may be painless and heal poorly.
  • Check your feet and between your toes regularly; ask for help if you cannot inspect them easily.
  • Wear well-fitting footwear, avoid walking barefoot and do not cut corns yourself with sharp tools.
  • A new wound, redness, swelling, discharge, blackened skin or a suddenly hot swollen foot needs prompt medical assessment. Fever or feeling very unwell calls for urgent care.
